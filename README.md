## ✨ Features

- ⏳ Time-Series Analysis: Dedicated tracking of monthly profit and loss metrics to reveal seasonal sales behaviors.
- 💳 Payment Gateway Distribution: Clear view into customer payment preferences (COD, UPI, Debit Card, Credit Card, EMI).
- 📍 Geospatial Insights: Breakdown of performance across top states including Maharashtra, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, and Gujarat.
- 🎯 Targeted Demographics: Tracking of elite customer spendings and distribution across product sectors.

## 🛠️ Data Architecture & Visualizations

The report houses key analytical components optimized for immediate executive overview:
- Profit-Loss by Month: A combo/bar visualization detailing monthly profitability shifts (noting high performance spikes in November).
- Quantity by Category: Donut chart analyzing product segments (Clothing leads at 62.62%, followed by Electronics at 20.55% and Furniture at 16.83%).
- Quantity by PaymentMode: Donut chart tracking transacting methods (COD leads with 44%, followed by UPI at 21%).
- Profit by Sub-Category: Horizontal bar chart sorting micro-level performance led by Printers and Bookcases.
- Top Customers: Visualizing the highest value accounts (e.g., Harivansh, Madhav, Madan Mohan, Shiva).
